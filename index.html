01 / THE IDEA

Not a map.
A decision engine.

Human-designed digital experiences use motion to communicate hierarchy, not to decorate it. MED-ROUTE treats every transition, signal and colour change as part of the operational story.

01 — ROUTE
↗

Shortest viable path.

Dijkstra + binary min-heap evaluates weighted roads while respecting live closure state. Fast is useful. Feasible is the goal.

02 — RESOURCES
+

Capacity changes the answer.

Specialty, beds and medicine are evaluated before a hospital becomes a destination. The closest building is not always the right one.

03 — MOTION
◎

The interface tells the story.

Signals pulse, routes flow, decisions appear as a timeline, and the command panel responds to the same state the map is showing.
